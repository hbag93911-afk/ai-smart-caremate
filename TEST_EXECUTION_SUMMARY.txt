6. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

Joint Detection (8 required):
  - left_shoulder, right_shoulder
  - left_hip, right_hip
  - left_knee, right_knee
  - left_ankle, right_ankle
  
Confidence Threshold: 0.55 (all joints must meet)

Step Detection Algorithm:
  Condition: (LEFT_ANKLE.Y_change > 15px) AND (LEFT_KNEE.Y_change > 15px)
  Interval: Minimum 400ms between consecutive steps
  Filtering: 5-frame sliding window average

Metrics Calculation:
  1. Left-Right Balance: Interval difference based
  2. Gait Symmetry: Multi-factor (intervals + variance)
  3. Body Sway: Hip midpoint X-movement tracking
  4. Leg Lift Symmetry: Knee height comparison
  5. Posture Consistency: Trunk tilt standard deviation
  6. Shoulder Symmetry: Y-coordinate difference ratio
  7. Walking Speed: steps / elapsed_time
  8. Cadence: steps / minutes
